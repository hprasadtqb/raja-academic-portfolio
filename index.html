About Me

I am a biochemist and academic with over two decades of experience in teaching and research, specializing in cardiovascular biology and lipid metabolism. My research focuses on experimental hypertension, oxidative stress, and cardiac remodeling, with a strong emphasis on translational strategies for disease prevention and management. I am actively engaged in postgraduate teaching, interdisciplinary research, and the organization of academic programmes in life sciences.
